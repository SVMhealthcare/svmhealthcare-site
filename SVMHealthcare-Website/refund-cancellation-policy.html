Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how cancellations, refunds and service delivery work for bookings made with Sri Vijayamahanteshwara Healthcare (SVM Healthcare).

1. Cancelling a booking

  • Before the technician starts travelling to you: free cancellation. Any online payment is refunded in full.
  • After the technician is on the way, but before collection: you may cancel, but the home collection charge may be retained. The test charges are refunded.
  • After the sample is collected: the booking cannot be cancelled because the sample has been taken and processing has begun.

To cancel, call or message us on +91 89044 45404, or write to info@svmhealthcare.online, as early as possible.

2. When you get a full refund

  • We cancel the booking or cannot send a technician.
  • The sample is rejected because of our collection or handling error and a recollection is not possible or not acceptable to you.
  • You were charged twice, or charged more than the amount shown.

3. Recollection

If a sample is found unsuitable by the laboratory (for example haemolysed, clotted, leaking or insufficient), we will arrange a recollection. If the fault is ours, there is no extra charge.

4. No refund cases

  • The patient is absent or unreachable at the booked time and the technician has travelled to the address (the home collection charge may apply to a rebooking).
  • Reports already delivered for tests that were completed correctly.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank) within 5 to 7 working days. For cash payments, refunds are made by bank or UPI transfer to an account you give us. Your bank may take extra time to show the credit.

6. Service delivery

  • We provide a service, not a physical product. There is no shipping.
  • A technician visits your address in the slot you booked. Home collection is available Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and on holidays by appointment.
  • Reports are delivered digitally in the app, usually within about 6 hours of the sample reaching the lab; special tests take longer.
