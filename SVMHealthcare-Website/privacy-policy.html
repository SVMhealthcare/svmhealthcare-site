Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Sri Vijayamahanteshwara Healthcare (SVM Healthcare) ("SVM Healthcare", "we", "us") provides blood tests and home sample collection in Bengaluru through our website svmhealthcare.online and the BDConnect mobile apps for patients and technicians. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we protect it. By using our services you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Identity and contact details: name, mobile number, email address (optional), age, gender.
  • Booking details: tests ordered, referring doctor (optional), collection address, collection date and time.
  • Location: if you choose, your current location or a pasted map location for home collection. For our technicians, live location is collected while they are travelling to or working on a collection, so that you can see where they are.
  • Health information: test results and reports prepared for you by our partner laboratories.
  • Payment information: the amount, mode (cash, UPI, card) and status of payment. Online payments are processed by our payment partner Cashfree Payments. We do not see or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank login.
  • Device information: a notification token so we can send you alerts, and basic technical logs.
  • Account security: your password, stored only in encrypted (hashed) form.

2. How we use it

  • To book, route and carry out sample collection, and to deliver your reports.
  • To send booking updates: technician assigned, on the way, sample collected, report ready.
  • To verify your mobile number and keep your account secure.
  • To process payments, refunds and invoices.
  • To meet legal and medical record-keeping requirements and to improve our service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your health information for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • Our technicians see your name, phone, age, gender, address and the tests ordered, only for the collection assigned to them.
  • Partner laboratories that process your samples receive the details needed to run the tests and issue your report.
  • Cashfree Payments for online payments.
  • Google Firebase to deliver push notifications to your phone.
  • Authorities where the law requires it.

4. Location permission

The patient app asks for location only when you tap "Use my current location". You may instead type your address or paste a map link. You can withdraw location permission at any time in your phone settings.

5. Storage, security and retention

Your data is stored on secured servers with access limited to authorised staff. Login tokens are stored in hashed form and passwords are hashed. We keep booking and report records for as long as needed for your care, for medical-record practice and for legal obligations, and then delete or anonymise them. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

6. Your rights and choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent, or to stop notifications (you can also switch notifications off in phone settings). Some records may need to be retained as required by law. Write to us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

Our services are used by adults. A parent or guardian should book on behalf of a child, and we treat the details they give as theirs to share.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Continued use of our services means you accept the updated policy.
