About SVM Healthcare

Trusted Diagnostics, Delivered to Your Doorstep

Sri Vijayamahanteshwara Healthcare (SVM Healthcare) is a Bengaluru-based diagnostic service that brings professional laboratory testing to your home. Our trained phlebotomists collect samples at your doorstep, our partner laboratory network processes them under strict quality protocols, and your reports reach you digitally.

We believe accurate diagnosis should never mean long queues, travel or waiting. Whether it is a single routine test or a complete preventive health checkup, we make the experience simple, hygienic and dependable for you and your family.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to visit a lab?

No. We offer home sample collection only, so you never have to travel or wait. Our phlebotomist comes to you at your chosen time.

How soon will I get my report?

Routine test reports are ready in 6 hours. For special tests, reports are shared as per the test TAT — we will tell you at the time of booking.

Do I need to fast before the test?

Sugar and lipid tests usually need 8–12 hours of fasting (plain water is fine). We will guide you when you book.

Are you available on holidays?

Yes, on holidays we work by appointment. Message us on WhatsApp to confirm a slot.

How do I receive my report?

Your digital report is sent securely on WhatsApp and/or email.

Terms & Conditions

Please read these terms before booking a test. Effective October 2026.

  1. Home collection only. SVM Healthcare provides home sample collection only; there are no walk-in lab visits. Service hours are Mon–Sat, 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM. On holidays we work by appointment.
  2. Booking. A booking is confirmed only after our team confirms your slot. Please give your correct name, phone number and address so our phlebotomist can reach you.
  3. Preparation and fasting. A fasting period of 10–12 hours is recommended for tests that need it (such as blood sugar and lipid tests). Please follow the instructions given at the time of booking.
  4. Prices and offers. MRP and offer prices shown on this website are indicative and are confirmed at the time of booking. Package prices are valid for a limited time and may be revised.
  5. Reports. Reports for routine tests are shared within about 6 hours. Special tests are reported as per the test's turnaround time (TAT). Reports are shared digitally on WhatsApp and/or email. Delays can occur due to technical or sample-related reasons.
  6. Sample quality. If a sample is insufficient, hemolysed or otherwise unsuitable, we may need to collect it again and will inform you.
  7. Medical disclaimer. Test reports are for information only and are not a substitute for medical advice. Please consult your doctor for interpretation of your results and for any treatment decisions.
  8. Partner network. Certain special tests and imaging services may be performed through our partner network. Radiology & Imaging services are coming soon.
  9. Payments, cancellations and refunds. Payment, rescheduling and cancellation terms are shared at the time of booking. For any questions, call or WhatsApp +91 89044 45404.
  10. Privacy. Your personal and medical information is kept confidential and is used only to provide diagnostic services to you.
  11. Changes. We may update these terms from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.
