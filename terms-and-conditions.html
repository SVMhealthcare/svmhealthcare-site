1. Our service

We offer blood tests and other diagnostic tests with home sample collection in Bengaluru. Samples are collected by our trained technicians and processed by our laboratory partners. Radiology and imaging services are not yet available.

2. Your account

3. Booking and collection

  • Choose your tests, date and time slot. Slots are subject to technician availability.
  • Be available at the address during the slot. Our technician will share a collection code with you at the visit; give it only to the technician at your door.
  • Follow the preparation instructions, for example 10 to 12 hours of fasting where advised, for accurate results.
  • A home collection charge applies to home visits and is shown before you confirm. Test prices shown are the prices payable, inclusive of the offers displayed at the time of booking.

4. Payment

You can pay in cash to the technician on collection, or online by UPI or card through Cashfree Payments where offered. Payments are processed securely by Cashfree; we do not store your card or UPI details. Prices may change; the price shown when you confirm the booking applies to that booking.

5. Reports

  • Routine reports are usually ready in about 6 hours of the sample reaching the laboratory; special tests take longer according to their turnaround time.
  • Reports are available in your app and downloadable as PDF once ready.
  • If a sample is unsuitable (for example haemolysed, clotted or insufficient), we will tell you and arrange a recollection.
